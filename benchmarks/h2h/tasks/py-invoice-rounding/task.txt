Customers are complaining that our invoices (the billing package) are off by a cent or more. An invoice with one line of 3 x 19.99 at 8.25% tax shows a subtotal of 59.94 instead of 59.97 and the wrong tax and total, a single item priced 0.29 comes out as 0.28, and when several small lines are taxed the tax does not match what you get adding up each line's tax. Please fix it so these are the rules everywhere: a unit price is converted to cents from the decimal value it is written as (19.99 is exactly 19.99, never its float approximation), rounding half up to the nearest cent (0.125 becomes 0.13); a line's amount is qty times that unit price in cents; the subtotal is the sum of the line amounts; tax is computed separately for each line as that line's amount in cents times the rate percent (8.25 means 8.25%), rounded half up to a whole cent (2.5 cents becomes 3 cents, not 2), and the invoice tax is the sum of those per-line taxes; the total is subtotal plus tax. So 3 x 19.99 at 8.25% must give subtotal 59.97, tax 4.95 and total 64.92. render() keeps its exact current layout and just shows the corrected numbers, and Invoice's methods (subtotal_cents, tax_cents, total_cents, render) and Line's unit_cents and amount_cents keep their names and return types.

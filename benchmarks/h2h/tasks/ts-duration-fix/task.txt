parseDuration in src/duration.ts is wrong for anything but the simplest input. "1h30m" comes back as just one hour, and "250ms" gets read as 250 minutes. It should accept any sequence of number+unit segments using d, h, m, s and ms (like "2d4h", "1m30s", "1h30m15s500ms"), with optional whitespace around and between segments, and return the total in milliseconds. Anything it can't parse completely should throw instead of being half-read: empty or blank strings, unknown units, trailing junk like "10mx", and a bare number with no unit.

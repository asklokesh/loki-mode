Add snake_case(text) to textkit/transforms.py. It splits camelCase and PascalCase words (an upper-case run followed by a capitalized word splits before that word), treats spaces, hyphens and underscores as separators, lowercases everything and joins the parts with single underscores, with none at either end: "HelloWorld" gives "hello_world", "parseHTTPResponse" gives "parse_http_response", "already_snake" stays the same, " Some Words-here " gives "some_words_here" and "" gives "".

Add truncate(text, width, suffix="...") to textkit/transforms.py, next to slugify and title_case. Text of at most width characters is returned unchanged; longer text is cut so that the result, suffix included, is exactly width characters: truncate("Hello world", 8) is "Hello...", truncate("Hello world", 8, suffix="~") is "Hello w~" and truncate("Hi", 8) is "Hi". A width smaller than len(suffix) raises ValueError("width must be at least the suffix length").

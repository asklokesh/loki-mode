Add a percent-off pricing rule, following the same pattern as the existing bulkDiscountRule in src/rules/bulkDiscount.ts (a factory that validates its arguments and returns a PricingRule from src/rules/types.ts, used by priceCart in src/pricing.ts). Create src/rules/percentOff.ts exporting percentOffRule(category: string, percent: number): PricingRule. The factory throws a RangeError unless percent is an integer from 1 to 100. The returned rule takes percent percent off the combined list price (unitCents times qty) of every line item whose category is exactly equal to category (case-sensitive), ignoring the running total, and rounds the discount half up to a whole cent (2.5 cents becomes 3); it returns { rule: "percent-off:<category>", amountCents: -discount } with a negative amount, or null when the discount is 0 (no matching items, or a discount that rounds to 0). Example: shirts lines of 3 x 1999 and 1 x 2500 at 15% give a discount of 1275 cents (1274.55 rounded).

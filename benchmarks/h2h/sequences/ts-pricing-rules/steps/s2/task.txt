Add a buy-X-get-Y-free pricing rule, following the same pattern as the existing rules in src/rules/ (bulkDiscountRule in bulkDiscount.ts and percentOffRule in percentOff.ts: a factory that validates its arguments and returns a PricingRule from src/rules/types.ts, used by priceCart in src/pricing.ts). Create src/rules/buyXGetY.ts exporting buyXGetYRule(sku: string, buy: number, free: number): PricingRule. The factory throws a RangeError unless buy and free are both positive integers. The returned rule adds up the qty of every line item whose sku is exactly sku (case-sensitive, lines pooled together), and for every complete group of buy + free units, free units cost nothing; the free units are priced at the lowest unitCents among those lines. It returns { rule: "buy-<buy>-get-<free>:<sku>", amountCents: -discount } with a negative amount, or null when there is no complete group or no such sku. The running total is ignored. Examples: buy 2 get 1 on 7 tees at 1500 cents gives two free tees, { rule: "buy-2-get-1:TEE", amountCents: -3000 }; buy 3 get 2 on 10 units gives 4 free units; buy 2 get 1 on 2 units gives null.

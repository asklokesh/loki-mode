Add formatCurrency(cents: number, symbol = "$"): string to src/format.ts. It formats a whole number of cents with thousands separators and two decimals: 123456 gives "$1,234.56", 5 gives "$0.05", 0 gives "$0.00", -500 gives "-$5.00", and formatCurrency(123456789, "€") gives "€1,234,567.89". A value that is not an integer (for example 1.5) throws RangeError("cents must be an integer").

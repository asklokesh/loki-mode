Add formatDuration(seconds: number): string to src/format.ts, next to formatBytes and formatPercent. Whole seconds (fractions are dropped with Math.floor) are shown as hours, minutes and seconds with no leading zero units: 3723 gives "1h 02m 03s", 125 gives "2m 05s", 45 gives "45s" and 0 gives "0s"; minutes and seconds after a larger unit are two digits. A negative value throws RangeError("seconds must be non-negative").

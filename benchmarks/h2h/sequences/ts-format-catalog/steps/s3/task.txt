Add formatCompact(n: number): string to src/format.ts. Below 1000 in absolute value it returns String(Math.round(n)) (950 gives "950"). From 1000 it divides by 1e3, 1e6 or 1e9 (the largest that fits) and appends K, M or B, with one decimal place and a trailing ".0" removed: 1000 gives "1K", 1200 gives "1.2K", 15300 gives "15.3K", 2500000 gives "2.5M", 7000000000 gives "7B"; a negative number keeps its "-" sign (-1200 gives "-1.2K").
